## Explicitly not collected in R0

Names, age, email, identity tokens, profiles, allergies, preferences, consent, wellness questions, meals, images, nutrients, goals, plans, progress, routes, coordinates, nearby searches, pose frames/landmarks, provider prompts/results, analytics identifiers, and advertising data.

Before any later field is persisted, add a complete row covering purpose, approved basis, collection, storage, encryption, roles, export, deletion, retention, backup, transfer, training, and logging. No production start occurs before a named human approves the retention matrix.
